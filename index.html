Before you apply

  • Never pay a fee to apply, to get "training," or to receive equipment. Legitimate VA roles do not charge candidates.
  • Be wary of pay offered only through a third-party app, or of anyone who asks you to move the conversation to Telegram or WhatsApp before a call.
  • Check the company name and the posting on the company's own site.
  • Listings change quickly. Confirm the role is still open on the board before you apply.
